A working network is not enough: you also need to be able to **reach** it when it is broken,
to **know** what is happening before users call, and to provide the **services** everything else
depends on (names, addresses, time). This hop groups these operations topics, the ones people often
discover on the day of an incident.

## What you will learn

| Chapter | What you will be able to do |
|---|---|
| **DDI: DNS, DHCP and IPAM** *(coming soon)* | Understand the three services and why they are managed together |
| **Management and out-of-band access** | Separate the management network, set up real out-of-band access and secure it |
| **SNMP, syslog and NetFlow** *(coming soon)* | Monitor device health, centralise logs and see who uses the bandwidth |
| **Telemetry with gNMI and gNMIc** *(coming soon)* | Stream device state continuously instead of polling it |
| **Prometheus and Grafana** *(coming soon)* | Store these metrics, build dashboards and alerts |

You can read the chapters in any order: they are independent.
